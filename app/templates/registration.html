Tournament registration and setup

Resume an unfinished tournament

Open a saved tournament to continue its bracket and board assignments.

1. Register players

Register each player once. The draw pairs players into two-person teams.

2. Number draw

The larger group draws first (females first when tied). The other group draws from those numbers. Matching numbers form mixed teams. If exactly two players are unmatched, they automatically become partners; four or more unmatched players draw again.

Draw-off

If exactly two players are unmatched, they automatically become partners. With four or more unmatched players, each draws again; every new number must appear exactly twice.

3. Review teams

Confirm the pairings. Bracket seeds are assigned automatically from the original draw numbers.

4. Bracket seeding

All four boards are always available. The operator assigns each bracket-ready match to a board.

Teams are ordered by their original draw number. If two unmatched players become partners, their team uses the lower of their two original numbers. The JSON bracket determines match order and routing.
