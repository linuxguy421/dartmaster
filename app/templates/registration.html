DARTMASTER

Tournament registration and setup

1. Register players

Register each player once. The draw pairs players into two-person teams.

2. Number draw

The larger group draws first (females first when tied). The other group draws from those numbers. Matching numbers form mixed teams; all unmatched players redraw to determine their partners.

Draw-off

Each unmatched player draws again. Enter the new number; each number must appear exactly twice.

3. Review teams

Confirm the pairings before assigning bracket positions.

4. Assign bracket seeds

Assign each team to a seed. The JSON bracket determines the match order and routing.
